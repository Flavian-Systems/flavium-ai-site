A VM decides where the agent runs. Flavium decides what it may do.

01 · The problem

Agents are hired for their authority, not their compute — they hold credentials to email, files, databases, and money. Prompt injection makes every agent a confused deputy: an attacker doesn’t need to escape a sandbox when the agent will misuse authority it legitimately holds. VMs and sandboxes protect the machine; Flavium protects the mission.

02 · Four pillars

I

Authority

Unforgeable grants with argument-level constraints.

II

Visibility

Per-agent namespaces.

III

Quantity

Budgets enforced mid-execution.

IV

Accountability

Hash-chained, replayable trace.
